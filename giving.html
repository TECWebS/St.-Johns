Tithes & Offerings — What's the Difference?

A common question, answered simply.

A Tithe

[Replace with your church's teaching.] A tithe traditionally refers to giving the first 10% of one's income back to God, given regularly as an act of obedience and trust — rooted in passages like Malachi 3:10.

An Offering

[Replace with your church's teaching.] An offering is any additional gift given above the tithe — whether toward general ministry, a building fund, missions, or a special need — given freely and cheerfully (2 Corinthians 9:7).

However you give, when you donate online you'll be able to note whether your gift is a tithe or an offering — update the PayPal form above with an "item options" dropdown for this once your account is set up.
